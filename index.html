Common questions

Is my meeting confirmed when I submit?

No. A request is not confirmed until Faisal approves the appointment.

Where can I find my appointment ID?

Your ID appears in the confirmation window after you submit. Keep it for your reference.

Will Faisal receive my request?

Not in this demo. Requests stay in this browser and are not sent to Faisal or emailed.

What time zone should I use?

Choose the time shown on your device. Confirm the time zone with Faisal before the meeting.
